## Key Distinction

Protists are NOT proto-primals. They do not evolve into primals. They are
**products that primals compose into**. The primals are the organisms; the
protists are what the organisms build.

`protists/footPrint` is the first entry. It is a browser-based GIS tool whose
Express backend will be replaced by a primal composition (petalTongue + nestGate
+ songBird). The browser frontend stays TypeScript — that's what browsers run.
